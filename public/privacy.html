Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Sijitek Solutions (sijiteksolutions.com)

Sijitek Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates RoofCalc Pro at https://sijiteksolutions.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our roof estimation, 2D/3D CAD design, paper sketch scanning, and quotation services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Google Sign-In Information: When you sign in with Google or email/password via Supabase Authentication, we receive only your basic profile information: your name, email address, and unique account identifier. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, or other Google account data.
  • Subscription & Billing Information: Pro upgrades are processed securely by Paystack. We store your subscription status, plan expiration date, and Paystack customer reference. We never see or store your credit/debit card numbers or bank credentials.
  • Uploaded Sketches & Project Inputs: If you use our optional Pro AI Paper Sketch Scanner, your uploaded sketch image is transmitted securely to our backend and processed via Google Gemini API solely to extract wall geometry and dimensions for your current session. Project dimensions and custom unit prices are stored locally in your browser storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and display your profile inside RoofCalc Pro.
  • To activate, verify, and manage your RoofCalc Pro subscription and monthly AI scan quota.
  • To process paper sketch images you explicitly upload for 2D/3D roof layout conversion.
  • To respond to customer support requests and maintain service security and reliability.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers to run RoofCalc Pro:

  • Supabase: User authentication and Pro subscription profile storage.
  • Paystack: Secure payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google Gemini API: Processing uploaded sketch images for Pro AI auto-dimensioning.
  • Vercel: Secure web hosting and serverless API delivery.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account profile and subscription status for as long as your account remains active. You may log out at any time from the top-right profile menu, clear local design data in your browser, or request permanent deletion of your account and profile records by emailing sijiteksub@gmail.com.

5. Security

All communication between your browser, RoofCalc Pro, Supabase, and Paystack is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to server-side keys and database administration is strictly restricted.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
